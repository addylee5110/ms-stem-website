A nonprofit for curious hands

Every girl deserves a hands-on STEM moment.

MS. stem runs free, volunteer-led, hands-on STEM workshops for middle school girls — girl-only, mentor-led, and designed to reach classrooms and community centers around the world. MS. stem Nonprofit Organization is fiscally sponsored by The Hack Foundation (d.b.a. Hack Club), a 501(c)(3) nonprofit (EIN: 81-2908499)

What we run

Free workshops, and everything a new chapter needs to start one.

01

After-School Workshops

Free, once-a-month, mentor-led sessions run by high school girls with local partner schools and community centers, girl-only by design.

02

Workshop Activity Plans

A detailed library of workshop ideas for chapter heads — each one with step-by-step instructions and the "why" behind it, so a brand-new chapter can run a great session on day one.
